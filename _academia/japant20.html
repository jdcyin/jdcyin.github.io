The goal

Health coverage for everyone by 2030

Universal health coverage is a priority for the G20, the World Health Organization and the UN. On current trajectories, four areas are unlikely to progress fast enough to meet the target.

The goal

Written for the G20

The brief was prepared by a working group convened by Japan, as host of the 2019 Think 20 summit, and published in The BMJ ahead of the G20 leaders' meeting in Osaka.

1 · Leaving no one behind

Primary care for ageing populations

Strong primary care reduces inequity. As populations age, it needs to shift towards the sustained care that non-communicable diseases require.

1 · Leaving no one behind

Migrants are a neglected challenge

Migrant workers should get similar access to health and social security as local workers, with benefits that travel with them when they return home.

1 · Leaving no one behind

The G20 is where many of them work

As major destination countries, G20 members are well placed to lead on shared migration data and on the health of migrant workers.

2 · Financing

Reliable money, spent with discipline

Health systems need reliable domestic financing, and finance and health ministries working together. Japan offers one model.

2 · Financing

Making every resource count

Affordable, good-quality medicines matter, especially antimicrobials, where misuse drives resistance. UHC and resistance plans can work towards the same policies.

3 · Innovation

Harness digital health, but regulate it

Digital health could transform access to care. Governments need rules in place before it reaches a tipping point, and we proposed a G20 working group to set them.

4 · Cooperation

Harmonise development aid

As the largest providers of development assistance, G20 members should coordinate their support, avoiding duplication and filling gaps.

4 · Cooperation

Monitor and learn together

Common monitoring and shared learning platforms let countries compare progress and learn what works, how and why.

How it was written

A global working group

A working group of researchers and policymakers from across Asia, Africa, Europe and North America, with input from civil society, refined the brief through a modified Delphi process.

I compiled and edited the overall draft with Gabriel Leung and created the graphics.
